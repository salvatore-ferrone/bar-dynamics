# Bar Dynamics: A Pedagogical Primer

This is a learning-in-public project on the dynamics of galactic bars, and how
they shape the morphology of stellar streams. It starts from the basics —
periodic orbits, surfaces of section, resonances — and builds toward
cutting-edge topics like resonance sweeping in slowing bars and stream
trapping/kinking.

Each chapter is a runnable Jupyter notebook. Code, figures, and animations
live alongside the physics explanations so the reasoning can always be
checked against a live calculation.
